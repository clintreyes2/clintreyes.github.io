Modeling vortex shedding in linear solvers

Modeling ocean structures, wave energy converters, and coastal structures typically use linear models to provide guided decision making for the design of such structures and devices. However, linear models are generally first order approximations and typically neglect energy dissipation due to vortex shedding.

Previous research have focused on quantifying this energy loss, and implemented such in linear solvers using a porous region. By defining a porous region, we are given two unknown parameters (1) the spatial extent and (2) the dissipation incurred in this region. However a detailed framework on determining these two parameters is still unavailable in the literature.
